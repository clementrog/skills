# Cases

What the source crits showed, for calibration. Read a case when you want to see what a finding with a rough after looks like. You don't need them for every crit. The crits are Brian Lovin's, from his public [Crit series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLJu44Klx1pB_8GSOUeDNDllPICvMJKSut) on YouTube. Each heading links to its video; the frames are described in words here, so watch the video to see them.

## What "rough" looks like

From the [Placemark crit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=reWeNx9KA_E), before and after.

The after keeps the real map, the real property names and the app's own styling. Every toolbar icon is a grey circle, with one purple square for the active tool. Nobody looking at it can argue about icons, so the two structural moves are all there is to see: two bands instead of one mixed strip, and a panel that starts below the tool band and fills its height. Aim for this fidelity in step 5.

## Scope and prominence: [Placemark map editor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=reWeNx9KA_E)

Before: one strip holds Menu, the document title, Feedback, the account avatar and the Feature / Table / Visual tabs; the drawing tools sit just under it. The properties list scrolls inside a short box, with Export and GeoJSON below and blank space under that.

After: top band for document and account, second band for editor tools, panel below it. Only Feature and Table remain as tabs, each with a tab shape. Properties fill the panel; Export and GeoJSON are collapsed rows at the bottom.

## Consequence: [Outline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UjG4lB2u-r4), adding a person to a group

Step 1, right after clicking Add: the list reads "No people left to add" and a toast says the person was added. Nothing on screen shows the group's members.

Step 2, one Back later: the "Group members" screen finally shows the person.

This is what a walked step looks like as evidence: two screenshots and the click between them. No single screenshot shows the problem.

## Context: [Outline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UjG4lB2u-r4), deleting a document

The confirmation is a full page. The document name is in bold in the sentence, but the document itself and the sidebar are gone. The designer proposed a small dialog over the dimmed document; he showed no mock, so there is no after.

## Signifier: [Outline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UjG4lB2u-r4) sidebar row

The hovered row is one highlighted strip, with a triangle at its left and "+" and "…" at its right. The triangle expands, the label navigates, and the highlight does not tell them apart.

## Scan lines, and an unresolved after: [Outline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UjG4lB2u-r4) Home

Before: titles align; stars trail each title at a different x; the grey metadata line has a different length on every row.

After: stars in their own column at the left, collection path in color under each title, avatars in a right-hand column, pinned documents as rows above the tabs. The designer said on camera that the two sections still feel detached and that he did not have the fix, and that the colored paths might be too much. An after can be reported as unresolved.

## Simplification loss: [Less Annoying CRM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9KMAudQBjE) contacts list

Original: dark two-column sidebar, blue names, striped rows.

First pass, the designer's first redesign: near-white, small dark names, thin grey icons.

Final: same structure as the first pass, with larger and darker text and colored navigation icons.

The first pass is the kind of screen a model produces by default. It was rejected for this product and this audience.

## Scope, constraints and click cues: [Less Annoying CRM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9KMAudQBjE) contact page

Original: the contact's actions (Edit contact, Assign to user, Merge duplicates, Print, Delete) sit in the second dark column beside global navigation. The lead is a green outlined box and the group is a pill, so both look clickable.

First pass: a full-width contact header with an Actions card under it. Sales leads and customer issues are plain text rows with a date.

Final: leads and issues are bounded cards again; section icons carry a color per category.

Two lessons in one page: the first pass fixed scope but moved things further than long-time users would accept, and it removed the cue that rows open.
